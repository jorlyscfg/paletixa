# La Paleti'Xa Public Landing Design

## Direction

The public landing is a tropical product showroom: a lively, image-led place to discover the current selection, not an operations dashboard with marketing copy attached.

## Visual language

- Use a full palette of coral, mango, mint, deep berry, and warm canvas tones through the existing theme tokens.
- Let local product photography carry the first impression; use solid color fields and offset shapes as framing, not as a replacement for imagery.
- Vary image scale, crop, section density, and corner treatment. Reserve rounded containers for purposeful image frames, actions, and catalog states.
- Keep display type heavy and compact, body copy readable, and labels short. Avoid gradient text and decorative dashboard chrome.

## Composition

- Lead with the verified antojo-to-business promise, live availability proof, and two clear visitor paths.
- Keep the catalog as the conversion center and retain its search, category, realtime, and recovery states.
- Use an image-led category story, a factual three-step orientation, honest FAQ answers, verified contact/social proof, and a closing CTA that returns to the catalog or wholesale portal.

## Interaction and performance

- Use one primary catalog action repeatedly; wholesale links are visually distinct secondary paths.
- Keep the first hero image local and eager; lazy-load below-fold authored images and reserve every media aspect ratio.
- Prefer restrained hover/focus motion and honor the existing reduced-motion rule.
